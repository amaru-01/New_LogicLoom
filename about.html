Who We Are

More Than an
IT Company

LogicLoom was founded in Nairobi with a clear vision — to bridge the technology gap facing Kenyan businesses.

Too many local companies were either overpaying for imported IT services or making do with substandard equipment and support. We set out to change that.

With a deeply local team and internationally certified engineers, we deliver world-class IT infrastructure solutions at prices that make sense for the Kenyan market.

Today, we serve over 500 clients across Nairobi, Mombasa, and Kisumu — from SMEs to large corporates — and we're just getting started.
